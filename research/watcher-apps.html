Each card shows what the Watcher's round screen would display, how the data flows, and where it runs. Teal steps stay on your network. Orange dashed steps call a cloud service. Screens are mockups, not captures from a real device.

ESP32-S3 · 8 MB PSRAM Himax HX6538 NPU 1.45″ 412×412 touch OV5647 · 120° FOV 1 mic · 1 W speaker Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz

One home stack, many apps

Every app below runs on the same two services on the RTX 3090 box. The voice units connect to a self-hosted XiaoZhi server; the camera unit pushes events to a small gateway.
