# FAQ and troubleshooting

### How do I connect to a different Wi-Fi network?

Hold the wheel to open the menu → **Settings → Wi-Fi setup**, or say "set up Wi-Fi again". The
device opens the **`Bap-XXXX`** hotspot; join it with your phone and pick the new network at
`http://192.168.4.1`. You can also add networks in the web settings (System tab) while still
connected.

### The screen says to check the network

The device keeps retrying. Check that your router is on 2.4 GHz (the ESP32-S3 has no 5 GHz) and
that the saved password is right.

### "Gemini rejected the session"

The key is missing, wrong or out of quota. Open `http://bap-xxxx.local` → System → Gemini and
paste a new key. With a paid fallback key the device switches by itself.

### It does not hear the wake word

Voice wake-up is **off by default** to save power: turn on *Voice wake-up* in the settings page or the wheel menu. Then say "Hi Lili" clearly, about a metre away. *Quiet nights* turns the wake word off from 22:00 to
06:00; press the wheel or double-tap the screen instead.

### It is too loud at night

Night mode (on by default) caps the volume at 35% from 22:00 to 06:00. Turn the wheel while it
talks, or set the volume in the menu or the web settings.

### I forgot the settings password

Hold the wheel for 20 seconds: the device resets all settings to defaults (Wi-Fi, key and password
included).

### How do I stop the music?

Double-tap the screen, say "stop the music", or press Stop in the web settings.

### Can I go back to the original firmware?

Yes. `firmware/restore_stock.sh` writes back your full backup. Without one, reinstall SenseCraft
with Seeed's tools; the factory keys at `0x9000` are untouched.

### Can I use it away from home?

The settings page is LAN-only by design. Use a VPN such as Tailscale or WireGuard to reach your
home network; do not expose the device to the Internet.

### Where are the logs?

Connect USB and open the serial port at 115200 baud, or use the conversation log in the Chat tab.
`/api/perf` shows CPU, memory and Wi-Fi health.
