## Zing MP3

Ask for a song or an artist: "play Hà Nội mùa thu", "some Sơn Tùng songs". `music.search` reads
back a short list; `music.play` streams the song and shows its **synced lyrics** (LRC) line by
line under the character's face. The face relaxes and sways while music plays.

## Radio

| Station | id |
| --- | --- |
| VOV1 News | `vov1` |
| VOV2 Culture and life | `vov2` |
| VOV3 Music | `vov3` |
| VOV Traffic Hanoi | `vovgt_hn` |
| VOV Traffic Ho Chi Minh City | `vovgt_hcm` |
| Hanoi FM 90 | `fm_hanoi_90` |
| Hanoi FM 96 | `fm_hanoi_96` |
| RFI Vietnamese | `rfi` |

Say "open VOV3", tap a station chip in the web settings, or pick **Radio** in the round menu.
Both MP3/AAC streams and HLS playlists are supported. The station list is data in
`assistant.json` (`radio[]`), so adding a station is a one-line change.

## SD card

Put `.mp3` files on a microSD card (Vietnamese file names are fine; FAT is mounted with UTF-8). Say
"play music from the card" or "play Diễm xưa from the card". Matching ignores accents and case, so
"diem xua" finds `Nhạc/Diễm Xưa - Khánh Ly.mp3`. No network is needed.

## Stopping

- Double-tap the screen (a single tap shows a hint first, so a stray touch never stops the music).
- Say "stop the music", or press **Stop** on the now-playing bar in the web settings.
