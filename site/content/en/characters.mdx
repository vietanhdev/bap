# Characters

Bắp ships with five characters. Each one has its own Gemini voice, personality and a face drawn
natively with LVGL: 23 expressions, blinking, breathing and a mouth that moves with the voice.

<Characters lang="en" />

## Switching characters

- **Wheel:** rotate while resting. The next character appears with its tagline.
- **Touch:** swipe sideways on the resting screen.
- **Voice:** "call Bắp", "let me talk to Bơ". The `character.switch` tool switches and restarts
  the session with the new voice and persona.
- **Menu:** hold the wheel → Characters.
- **Web settings:** the Personalization tab.

## Personalize without rebuilding

In the web settings you can rename a character, change its voice and rewrite its persona. The
edits are stored per device in NVS, so two devices can run the same firmware with different
personalities: for example, a short-and-funny Bơ on one desk and a gentle Bông on another. Empty
fields restore the built-in values.

## How a face is drawn

Characters are data in `firmware/data/assistant.json`. The `look` object picks a body shape
(`cloud`, `corn`, `avocado`, `mochi` or `robot`) and four colours (`body`, `accent`, `ink`,
`cheek`). `assistant_face.cc` builds the body, eyes, brows, mouth and extras from those values, so a
new character that reuses a shape needs no C++. See [Add a character](/en/adding-a-character).

At night the face goes calm: no breathing animation, a still "zzz", and only a handful of redraws,
which keeps LVGL around 1% CPU.
