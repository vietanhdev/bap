# Wheel and touch

![Wheel and touch gestures](/diagrams/controls.svg)

## Scroll wheel

| State | Gesture | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resting | Rotate | Volume |
| Resting | Double-press | Open the app list; rotate to choose, press to open |
| Talking | Rotate | Volume |
| Talking | Press | Stop or resume listening |
| Any | Hold 1 second | Open the menu (hold again to go back) |
| In the menu | Rotate / press | Move / open |
| Any | Press 3 times quickly | Power off |
| Any | Hold 20 seconds | Factory reset: erase all settings |

## Touch screen

| State | Gesture | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resting | Tap | The character giggles |
| Resting | Double-tap | Start talking |
| Resting | Hold | Open the menu |
| Speaking | Tap | Interrupt (keeps listening) |
| Talking | Double tap | End the conversation |
| Playing music | Double-tap | Stop the music |
| In the menu | Drag, or tap the item above/below | Move; tap the middle item to open; hold to go back |

## The round menu

Hold the wheel (or the screen) to open it: **Chat, Apps, Radio, Settings, Power off**.
An arc on the rim shows where you are, the current item sits in a pill and its neighbours fade
above and below.

**Settings** has volume and brightness sliders, the language, toggles for night mode, presence,
morning greeting, quiet nights and greeting people, device info, Wi-Fi setup and restart.
**Power off** asks for confirmation.
