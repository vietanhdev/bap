# Camera and smart features

The Watcher's camera has its own AI chip, a Himax HX6538, which runs a person detector **on the
chip**. Bắp uses it for small, private touches. No image is sent anywhere and no Gemini quota is
used.

| Feature | What happens | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Notice people** | When someone appears, the screen wakes up and the character looks happy | off |
| **Morning greeting** | The first time it sees you between 6:00 and 10:00, the character greets you and reads the morning briefing (weather, lunar date, headlines). Once a day; needs *Notice people* | off |
| **Greet people** | Says hello when someone arrives | off |
| **Night mode** | 22:00–06:00: dim screen, volume capped at 35%, calm face (no breathing, still "zzz") | on |
| **Quiet nights** | 22:00–06:00: the wake word is off, so TV or conversation cannot wake it. Double-tap the screen or press the wheel to talk | off |
| **Screen rest** | After 5 quiet minutes with nobody around, the screen dims to 6% and the face stays still to save power. A touch, the wheel, the wake word or a person walking by brings it back | on |
| **Low battery** | Reminds you to plug it in | on |

Turn them on in the web settings (System tab) or in the round menu → Settings.

<Callout type="info">
Camera photos only go to Gemini when the assistant calls `self.camera.take_photo`, for example in
the *Look and tell* app or when you ask "what am I holding?".
</Callout>

## Other sensors and hardware

- **Microphone array** with Espressif's AFE (noise suppression, echo cancellation) and the
  "Hi Lili" wake word model running on the device.
- **SD card** for offline music (shares the SPI bus with the camera).
- **Battery** level and charging state, shown in the status bar.
